Turn on ad & pop-up blocking in Safari (one-time setup)
  1. Settings → Apps → Safari → turn on Block Pop-ups.
  2. Same screen → Prevent Cross-Site Tracking and Hide IP Address (From Trackers).
  3. Install a free content blocker from the App Store (e.g. AdGuard or 1Blocker), then Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → turn it on.
  4. For zero history, use a Private tab: tap the tabs button → Private.
